Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you buy.

How do I open the tracker after downloading?
After purchasing on Etsy, download the HTML file and double-click it to open in your default browser. It works on any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox. No installation, no account, no internet required after download.
Does it really work offline?
Yes. The tracker is a standalone HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. Once downloaded, you can use it with no internet connection at all. Your data is stored locally in your browser.
Is my financial data safe?
Absolutely. Unlike cloud-based apps, no data ever leaves your device. There are no servers, no accounts, and no tracking. Everything is stored in your browser's local storage. We recommend exporting a JSON backup weekly from the Settings page.
Can I use it on my phone and my computer?
Yes. The tracker is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. To use the same data on two devices, export a JSON backup from one device and restore it on the other.
What happens if I clear my browser data?
Clearing your browser's cookies and site data will erase your tracker data. This is why we strongly recommend exporting a backup every week. The app even reminds you if a week passes without a backup.
Do I need to pay again for updates?
No. Your one-time purchase includes future updates at no extra cost. We'll notify buyers through Etsy when a new version is available.
